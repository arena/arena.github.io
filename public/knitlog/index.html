Design Choices

Fewer features, less friction

We chose to restrain ourselves from adding every possible feature to KnitLog in order to keep it streamlined for its core purpose — to save your place in your project. We wanted a quick, private, uninterrupted way to get to the next row in our pattern or jot down a thought.

For example, a note like “next row, knit across” is enough — the context is already there because it's a time-stamped log. Need a photo only you would understand? Just add it. Got personal information in the background? No big deal — it's as private as anything else on your phone. And with no public version of your project, you never have to edit yourself.

Elegant interactions

Fewer features doesn't mean a lot of work didn't go into the design. As users of KnitLog ourselves, we have improved the little details along the way.

Just downloaded a pattern PDF and want to get it into a new or existing project? Use the share feature and select KnitLog as the destination. Yay! Want a quick way into a handful of simultaneous projects? Add a KnitLog widget for each one to the Home Screen of your iPhone or iPad. We are continually improving the core features of KnitLog while avoiding clutter and complexity.

Craft-focused

Needles and hooks are easy to save to your project. Pattern lists needles in metric but you think in US sizes? We got you — just pick from the drop-down to save your needles or hooks and see the conversion.

Want to keep track of the dye lot in the yarn you're using? The fields for your yarn selections match standards for yarn attributes (Brand, Name, Colorway, Dye lot, etc.).

When you create a project in KnitLog, you set your future self up for success. If you put a project on hold, you can get back to where you were (and back to your working gauge with the right needle and yarn).
