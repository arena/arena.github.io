What KnitLog collects

No data collected. KnitLog doesn’t collect, store, or share any of your data, and there’s no analytics or tracking code in the app. That’s why the App Store lists KnitLog as “Data Not Collected.”

Where your data lives

Your projects, notes, photos, yarn, needles, and patterns live on your device and sync through your own private iCloud account, using Apple’s CloudKit. We don’t run a server, and we can’t see any of it. iCloud is covered by Apple’s privacy policy. KnitLog widgets read from a copy kept on the same device.

Accounts, ads and tracking

There’s nothing to sign up for. KnitLog uses the iCloud account already on your device. No ads, and no tracking across apps or websites.

Link previews

When a note includes a link, KnitLog shows a preview of that page (its title and image). Your device fetches the preview directly from the website, just as Safari would. That website sees a normal visit; we don’t see anything. Previews aren’t saved; they’re only kept while the app is open.

Purchases

Any purchase in KnitLog is made through the App Store and handled by Apple. We never see your name, Apple Account, or payment details.

Crash reports

KnitLog doesn’t send crash reports. If you’ve turned on Share With App Developers in your iPhone or iPad settings (Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements), Apple may share anonymous crash information with us. You can turn that off at any time.

Children’s privacy

KnitLog doesn’t collect personal information from anyone, including children.

When you contact us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to send, such as screenshots or a pattern file. We use it only to reply to you, we never share it, and we’ll delete it if you ask.

Our Discord server is run by Discord, so Discord’s privacy policy applies there, and other members can see what you post.

If you’re testing a KnitLog beta through TestFlight, Apple shares crash reports and usage information from the beta with us, along with any feedback or screenshots you send through TestFlight. If you were invited by email, we also see your name and email address; if you joined through a public link, we don’t. See TestFlight & Privacy.

Deleting your data

Deleted projects go to Recently Deleted for 30 days, then they’re removed from all your devices. You can empty the trash sooner. To delete everything KnitLog has stored in iCloud, delete KnitLog’s data from your iCloud storage settings. Apple’s guide to managing iCloud storage shows where to find them. Deleting the app removes its data from that device.

Changes to this policy

The “Last updated” date at the top of this page changes whenever the policy does. If a new version of KnitLog ever handles data differently, this policy is updated before that version ships. The App Store privacy label is kept in step with this page. Every past version of this policy is public: see its history on GitHub.
